Embark on an Unforgettable Highland Adventure

Combining raw nature, rich colonial history, and world-famous tea heritage

This journey takes you deep into the heart of the hills through the stunning Pekoe Trail Stages 8 & 9. Walk along endless carpets of lush green tea estates, pass through charming local villages, and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the scenic Castlereagh and Maskeliya Reservoirs.

The adventure reaches its ultimate peak at Horton Plains National Park, a misty, high-altitude paradise. Hike through unique cloud forests and montane grasslands leading straight to the dramatic World’s End sheer cliff drop and the cascading beauty of Baker’s Falls. Along the way, experience the cozy luxury of staying in a heritage tea bungalow in Norwood, surrounded by chilly mountain weather.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Pick up from Colombo by your private chauffeur. Transfer into the misty hills of Norwood. En route, optional White Water Rafting can be enjoyed in Kithulgala. Check into your heritage tea bungalow and settle into the chilly mountain ambiance.

🥾 Pekoe Trail Stage 8 🌿 Tea Plantation Trek
Overnight: Norwood — Tea Queen Bungalow
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 2 of 2

Morning Take on **Pekoe Trail Stage 9**, journeying deeper through breathtaking highland scenery.

Afternoon Proceed to Nuwara Eliya, known as 'Little England', and check in to your tranquil villa surrounded by mountain views.

🥾 Pekoe Trail Stage 9 🚗 Scenic Drive
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — Tranquil Villa
